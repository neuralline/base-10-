# Base-10 Timer

The Base-10 Timer is an exciting exploration of time measurement, offering a fresh perspective on how we track and understand time.
Whether you're a developer, a time enthusiast, or just curious about new concepts, we invite you to explore the Base-10 Timer and its
unique features! This innovative timer reimagines the way we perceive and measure time by utilizing a decimal-based system. Instead of the traditional 60 Seconds and 60 Minutes the Base-10 Timer operates on a base-10 scale. Fun and Interesting project using Cyer for automation

## Time for reimagine!

Have you ever wondered why we divide time into awkward units of 60 seconds, 60 minutes, and 24 hours? In a world where decimal systems dominate every other form of measurement, our traditional timekeeping remains a curious relic of ancient Babylonian mathematics.

Imagine a world where time is as intuitive as counting in tens! The Base-10 Timer simplifies time measurement, making it accessible for everyone. By converting time into a decimal format, it aligns with our natural counting system, enhancing comprehension and usability.

## How It Works

The Base-10 Timer divides time into the following units:

- **1 Second**: Remains the same as the standard second.
- **1 Minute**: Equivalent to 100 seconds (approximately 1.7 standard minutes).
- **1 Hour**: Equivalent to 100 minutes (approximately 2.8 standard hours).
- **1 Day**: Remains the same as the standard 24 hours.

### Key Calculations

- **Decimal Hours per Day**: The timer calculates decimal hours based on the total seconds elapsed since midnight, converting it into a decimal format.
- **Standard Time Display**: Alongside the base-10 time, the timer also displays the standard time for reference.

## Features

- **Real-Time Updates**: The timer updates every second, providing a live display of the current base-10 time and standard time.
- **Progress Tracking**: A progress bar visually represents the percentage of the day that has passed, allowing users to see how much time remains.
- **Detailed Stats**: The timer provides additional statistics, including:
  - Base-10 hour
  - Standard hours
  - Day progress percentage
  - Time scale comparison (1 hour in base-10 = 2.78 standard hours)
